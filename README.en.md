## 🎬 Practical tools

### [Feiyue Video Tools](https://sasafreya.github.io/feiyue-video-tools/)

**Leave repetitive video packaging to the tool, and make more time for creating.**

Process videos locally: resize, replace backgrounds, add logos, watermarks and end cards, save reusable presets, standardize filenames, and export multiple packaging variations.

[Website & demo ↗](https://sasafreya.github.io/feiyue-video-tools/) · [Downloads](https://github.com/SasaFreya/feiyue-video-tools/releases) · [Project documentation](https://github.com/SasaFreya/feiyue-video-tools)

<sub>macOS / Windows · Local processing · The public repository contains documentation and downloads; desktop application source code is not public.</sub>

### [Feiyue Planner](https://sasafreya.github.io/feiyue-planner/)

**Keep the year in view, and finish today one thing at a time.**

Paper-inspired yearly, monthly, and weekly planning, project calendars, a floating desktop checklist, and actual time tracking—with room for notes and small thoughts.

[Website & demo ↗](https://sasafreya.github.io/feiyue-planner/) · [Downloads](https://github.com/SasaFreya/feiyue-planner/releases) · [Source & documentation](https://github.com/SasaFreya/feiyue-planner)

<sub>Currently available for macOS Apple Silicon.</sub>

## 🌷 Playful experiences

### [Little Creations](https://sasafreya.github.io/little-creations/)

**Small web creations to look at, touch, and play with.**

A collection of botanical and interactive experiments. Not every idea needs to become a full product; sometimes making it is enough.

| Creation | Experience |
| --- | --- |
| [Lotus Calendar](https://sasafreya.github.io/little-creations/lotus-calendar/) | A poster-like annual calendar with year switching, date notes, and printing. |
| [A Letter in Flowers](https://sasafreya.github.io/little-creations/flower-letter/) | A bouquet dissolves into particles and reforms as a picture; try your own photo. |
| [Window Blooms](https://sasafreya.github.io/little-creations/window-blooms/) | Unfold flowers and adjust sunlight and breeze around a vase by the window. |
